HOW TO PLAY

🎯 Goal

Eliminate all enemy soldiers and stay inside the shrinking safe zone. Last one alive wins.

📱 Touch

Left joystick to move · drag right side to look · FIRE / AIM / JUMP / RUN / LOAD buttons · tap ✋ to pick up weapons, ammo and medkits.

⌨️ Desktop

WASD move · mouse look (click game to lock mouse) · click = fire · right mouse = aim · Shift run · Space jump · R reload · 1/2/3 weapons · E pickup · Esc pause.

🔫 Weapons

You start with a pistol. Find the Assault Rifle and Shotgun at the village, outpost, warehouse and hilltop. Watch your ammo!
